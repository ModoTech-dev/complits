Our process

How we work

Four simple steps, the same whether you need a banner by Friday or a new website for the year ahead.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, message or fill in the form. We'll ask a few questions about size, quantity, timeline or your current setup.

  2. Get a quote

    You receive a clear price and delivery date before any work begins. No surprises later.

  3. Approve the proof

    For print, you sign off a digital proof. For IT and web, you approve the plan and see progress along the way.

  4. Delivery & support

    We deliver or install, then stay available for reprints, updates and support.

FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

How quickly will you reply to my request?

We reply within one working day. For anything urgent, call or WhatsApp us on +254 703 626 310.

Can you handle both the printing and the IT side of a project?

Yes. We can design and print your branding, then build your website and set up your computers, so you deal with one team instead of several.
